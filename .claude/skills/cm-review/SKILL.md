# Review recovery evidence

Read `docs/agent/REVIEW.md` and execute both review axes for the supplied scope.
For example, review a C acceptance claim against its source/report identities
and remaining original-code boundaries. Report evidence and concrete findings;
a reviewer does not promote recovery states.

A standalone invocation follows `PLAYBOOK.md` preflight and closure; a delegated
reviewer inherits the parent's baseline and returns findings to the parent.
